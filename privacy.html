Privacy information — English

1. Data controller

JSK Composite — business project under development in Poland. Contact: JSK.COMPOSITE.OFFICE@gmail.com. Full registered identity and mailing address will be completed before commercial launch.

2. Purpose and legal basis

If you send an enquiry, we process your contact name, email, optional company, delivery country, requested materials and message to respond to you and discuss a possible B2B transaction. The anticipated GDPR legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) when taking steps prior to a contract, or Article 6(1)(f) for legitimate business interest in answering enquiries, subject to final legal review. The acknowledgement checkbox confirms you have read this notice; it is not consent to marketing.

Recruitment enquiries

For employer recruitment enquiries, we may process requested roles, industry, number of vacancies, contract type, start date and work location to reply to the company. This employer form is not intended for candidate CVs, personal identity documents or sensitive personal data; please do not submit them. Recruitment and staffing services are in development and subject to applicable EU and national requirements.

Training and qualification enquiries

For technical training requests we may collect the subject, location, number of participants, preferred delivery format, start date and expected qualification. We use these details solely to discuss training needs and possible options. Do not submit personal data about individual trainees, IDs, medical details or restricted technical information. Any formal certification is governed by its authorised issuer.

3. Service providers

Our website is hosted on Render. Form processing uses Web3Forms, spam protection uses hCaptcha and illustrative images come from Unsplash. These providers may process technical data, such as IP addresses. Some processing may occur outside the EEA. Their contractual terms, transfer safeguards and data-processing locations must be confirmed before full operation.

4. Retention and your rights

Enquiries are retained no longer than needed to reply and meet any applicable legal requirements. Final retention periods need to be specified before commercial operation. You may request access, correction or erasure where applicable, object to processing and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Contact the email above.

5. Cookies, tracking and marketing

We do not run our own analytics scripts or offer browser push subscriptions. hCaptcha and external services may use cookies and related technologies; this requires a final cookie/privacy assessment before launch.

6. IP-based language and saved preference

In automatic mode your browser requests your IP country from GeoJS (get.geojs.io), which receives your public IP address. We use only the returned country code to select Polish, German or English; we do not request GPS location. VPNs and roaming connections are classified by their public exit IP. Manual language selection is stored in localStorage (jsk_composite_lang_pref) until you select AUTO or clear browser data. If the country lookup fails, English is used. Hosting, form, anti-spam and image providers receive technical connection data. Provider terms and international transfer safeguards still require confirmation.

6. Język według IP i zapamiętany wybór

W trybie AUTO przeglądarka pobiera kraj adresu IP z GeoJS (get.geojs.io), które otrzymuje publiczny adres IP. Wykorzystujemy wyłącznie kod kraju do wyboru polskiego, niemieckiego lub angielskiego; nie pobieramy lokalizacji GPS. VPN i roaming są rozpoznawane według publicznego adresu wyjściowego. Ręczny wybór zapisujemy w localStorage (jsk_composite_lang_pref) do wybrania AUTO lub wyczyszczenia danych przeglądarki. Przy błędzie ustalania kraju stosowany jest angielski. Dostawcy hostingu, formularza, ochrony antyspamowej i zdjęć otrzymują dane techniczne połączenia. Warunki dostawców i zabezpieczenia transferów międzynarodowych wymagają jeszcze potwierdzenia.
